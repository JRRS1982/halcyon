The method

A few minutes a month, and the spreadsheet can go

Most budgets die around month three. Balanced keeps the part that works and does the rest.

Each month runs the same four steps:

  1. Import a statement, or write a line
  2. Approve what Balanced filed for you
  3. Read the budget against your plan
  4. Update balances and see where you stand

Then the plan carries it forward, decades ahead, with real UK tax bands.
