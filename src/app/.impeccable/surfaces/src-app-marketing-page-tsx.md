Scope: src/app/(marketing)/page.tsx and src/components/marketing/**. Mode: Persuade. Inherits the Market Pages world (DESIGN.md); this brief only settles composition.

Audience and job: a UK household running money in a spreadsheet, arriving cold. In under 60 seconds they must know what Balanced is, see it working, believe it is safe, and find the button. Owner's note (1 Nov 2026): the previous page felt disorganised, mixed in style and too complex; go further into the financial-newspaper front page, with articles that explain the features and show them.

Constraints: no invented claims, prices, testimonials or users; sample figures are labelled as samples. Keep #how, #features, #details anchors, the h1 "Make sense of your money.", "No bank connection, ever" and the "export or delete" link to /guide (specs rely on them).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The landing page is the front page of a financial paper whose subject is your money: a lead story that says what Balanced is, a market-data box that shows what it tells you, and inside articles that explain each feature with a picture of it. Refuses the SaaS template (centred hero, icon-card grid, timeline, stacked feature rows).

OWN-WORLD: Salmon paper, warm ink, claret and oxford. Source Serif 4 headlines at front-page scale, Libre Franklin body in short columns, hairline and double ink rules between stories, a tinted market-data box with ▲▼ figures, captions in italic serif. No boxes around stories, no kicker labels over headings.

STORY: The visitor reads the lead headline and one-line standfirst, glances at the sample month's figures, sees the product in the lead picture, reads the trust line, and either presses Get started or scrolls to an article that answers their question (where did it go, am I getting richer, how do statements get in, will it last).

FIRST VIEWPORT: Under the nav, a skybox of three teasers with thumbnails pointing to the articles below; a double ink rule and dateline; then a two-thirds lead story (h1, standfirst, Get started + Sign in, trust line, lead picture with caption) beside a one-third rail holding "Your month, at a glance" (sample market-data box) and "The monthly briefing" (the four steps, #how).
