## Example One

Name: Jeremy Smith
Age: 43
Occupation: Software Engineer
Frustrations: Inefficient financial tracking, lack of clear long-term financial visibility, and complex budgeting tools.
Location: London, UK
Income: £150,000
Education: University of London, BSc Computer Science
Tech literacy: Advanced
Personality traits: Detail-oriented, analytical, and future-focused
Favourite brands: Vanguard, Monzo, Google
Tagline: "I plan my code and my finances with equal precision."
Values: Financial security, efficiency, and continuous learning
Bio: Jeremy is a software engineer who values planning and foresight in both his professional and personal life. He's looking for a tool that can help him visualize his family's financial future while keeping track of their spending and savings.
Core Needs: A reliable way to track past expenses, plan future finances, and maintain a sustainable budget for his family.
Beliefs: That financial planning should be as systematic and well-structured as quality code, with clear metrics and predictable outcomes.
Goals: To achieve long-term financial stability, maximize savings, and make informed financial decisions for his family's future.
Motivations: The desire for financial clarity, the need to plan for his family's future, and the satisfaction of seeing his financial plans materialize.
Obstacles: Complex financial tools that are hard to maintain, lack of clear future projections, and difficulty tracking long-term financial trends.
Preferences: Clean, efficient interfaces that respect his time, with the ability to dive deep into financial data when needed.
Priorities: Financial security, long-term planning, and data-driven decision making.

## Example Two

Name: Margaret Wilson
Age: 72
Occupation: Retired School Teacher
Frustrations: Struggles with technology, finds financial terminology confusing, and worries about making mistakes with money.
Location: Manchester, UK
Income: £24,000 (pension)
Education: Teaching Certificate, 1972
Tech literacy: Basic - can use email and simple websites with help
Personality traits: Cautious, methodical, values security and simplicity
Favourite brands: M&S, BBC, Nationwide
Tagline: "I just want to know I'll be okay without having to understand all the complicated bits."
Values: Security, trust, and simplicity
Bio: Margaret taught primary school for 40 years but never felt comfortable with technology or financial matters. She's recently widowed and now has to manage her own finances for the first time in decades. She's worried about making mistakes that could affect her retirement.
Core Needs: A simple way to ensure her pension will last, understand her regular expenses, and not feel overwhelmed by financial complexity.
Beliefs: That managing money should be straightforward and not require understanding complex financial concepts.
Goals: To feel secure in her financial future, maintain her independence, and not be a burden on her family.
Motivations: Peace of mind, security, and maintaining her current lifestyle without stress.
Obstacles: Finds technology intimidating, gets confused by financial jargon, and worries about scams or making costly mistakes.
Preferences: Clear, simple interfaces with large text, minimal steps, and human support when needed.
Priorities: Security, ease of use, and having someone trustworthy to turn to with questions.

## Example Three

Name: Jake Thompson
Age: 24
Occupation: Barista/Part-time DJ
Frustrations: Never seems to have enough money despite earning decent tips, often surprised by bills, and struggles with impulse spending.
Location: Brighton, UK
Income: £22,000 (plus cash tips)
Education: College diploma in Music Technology
Tech literacy: Very comfortable with social media and mobile apps, but not with spreadsheets or financial tools
Personality traits: Spontaneous, social, present-focused, optimistic
Favourite brands: Nike, Spotify, Deliveroo, Monzo
Tagline: "I'll figure it out when I get paid next... right?"
Values: Freedom, experiences, social status, living in the moment
Bio: Jake loves his social life and keeping up with the latest trends. He's great at his jobs and makes decent money between the coffee shop and DJ gigs, but somehow it always seems to disappear. He knows he should save but finds it hard to say no to nights out with friends or the latest tech gadgets.
Core Needs: A way to control spending without feeling restricted, understand where his money actually goes, and save for big purchases without it feeling like a chore.
Beliefs: That life's too short to worry about every penny, but he should probably be a bit more responsible with money.
Goals: To save for a DJ equipment upgrade, go on a festival holiday with friends, and stop living paycheck to paycheck.
Motivations: Wants the freedom to enjoy life now while also securing some financial stability for bigger future goals.
Obstacles: Impulse purchases, peer pressure to spend, lack of financial education, and difficulty delaying gratification.
Preferences: Mobile-first, visually engaging interfaces with minimal text, gamification elements, and social features.
Priorities: Immediate needs and social life, with some attention to future goals when reminded.
